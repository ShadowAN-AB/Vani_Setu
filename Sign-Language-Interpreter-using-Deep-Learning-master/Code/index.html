About Vani-Setu

Vani-Setu (“voice-bridge”) is a camera-based sign language translator for classroom and accessibility demos. It tracks one or two hands with MediaPipe, recognises letters and signs with machine learning, speaks the result, and shows the sentence in English and Hindi.

College iteration

  • Letters A–Z with an SVM on 88 hand features (96.9% test accuracy), robust to the other hand and a tilted or distant hand.
  • A neural network (MLP) for ASL phrases and Indian Sign Language (ISL) signs once you record samples; rules until then.
  • Word prediction that can fix a misread letter, and a Dictionary showing how to make every sign.
  • A landmark dataset recorder with signer names and a test-set option, and retraining from the browser.
  • Offline Hindi fallback when Google Translate is unavailable.
  • SMTP secrets moved to .env. OTP prints in the terminal when email is not set.

Honest limits

  • Single frames only, so the motion letters J and Z are only partly recognised.
  • Phrase and ISL modes use rules until you record samples and retrain.
  • ISL here is a teaching approximation, not a certified ISL lexicon.
